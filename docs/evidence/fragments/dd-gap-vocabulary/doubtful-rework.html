Reworking the doubtful vocabulary additions

Seven worklist proposals map to seven DD source paths. Three distinct tokens are proposed for ruling: pellet_species, current_profile_alignment and plateau_factor. The other four rows compose existing quantities and retain explicit source bindings. No vocabulary is admitted and no source is promoted by this proposal.

Strict parsing and composition at main revision d46fd86214d7c8d3f9ee7ba46c3a93c37aa827f9 pass for all six existing-token spellings probed. Four positive parser controls and two known-present worklist controls pass. Nine earlier spellings or attempted decompositions still fail, with the refusals recorded in the companion JSON. Grammar files are unchanged.

The binding decisions are substantive. The grid-point subset, along-line subset and source-current contribution cannot be identified completely by their flat quantity names. Their recorded predicates, sample domains and source identities must remain part of the downstream binding identity. Accepting these splits requires the lead to accept that division of responsibility; a passing parser check does not settle it.

The machine-readable proposal contains every source path, exact worklist leaf and parent documentation, units, reused-token registration, required binding and parser result. The landing record records the measure.

The selection is encounters_townsend_condition OR is_closed_magnetic_field_line. The sample domain is tracing_grid_points, and the tracing grid is plasma_initiation/b_field_lines/grid. There is no existing Boolean-OR selector in the flat grammar, and the current geometry guard rejects non-plane prefix qualifiers. The reproduced breakdown_vertical_grid refusal is geometry carriers cannot carry non-plane prefix qualifiers; got ['breakdown']. The split therefore preserves the predicate explicitly in the binding rather than claiming that concatenating qualifiers expresses it.

Ruling requested: Accept the carrier/selection split only if the downstream source binding retains the explicit OR predicate and sample domain as identity.   • townsend_field_line: Conjoins words but cannot preserve the Townsend OR closed-line union.
  • breakdown_vertical_grid: breakdown selects a discharge phase, not the union predicate, and non-plane geometry prefix qualifiers are refused.

Proposed physical_base: current_profile_alignment. A global, current-valued figure of merit measuring spatial agreement among the current-density profiles supplying the plasma current, evaluated with the source-profile comparison and weighting prescribed by the DD-cited Wade et al. convention; it is not an angle, a current-density vector component, or an assumed dimensionless factor. Unit: A. This token is not registered.

The DD unit is A. Retain the Wade et al. current-profile convention with its weighting and normalization. Its DOI suffix is not a page number: the paper is Nuclear Fusion 43, 634–646. The cited full formula was not recovered; the proposal does not invent an integral, a monotonicity convention or a dimensionless normalization. Formula verification remains necessary before admission.

Ruling requested: Consider one named physical base current_profile_alignment rather than the qualifier current_alignment plus generic factor. Keep the cited convention normative and verify its full mathematical definition before vocabulary admission.

  • current_alignment_factor: Neither current nor alignment is an existing qualifier; generic factor does not establish this current-valued comparison or its weighting.
  • current: A total or component electric current does not describe agreement between source profiles.

Documentation is preserved from the assigned worklist, recorded against DD 4.1.0; the published DD reference cross-checks the quantity. Fresh DD-only MCP results confirm the source identity, but their enriched descriptions do not override the worklist documentation.

Proposed physical_base: plateau_factor. The dimensionless magnetic-configuration factor for plateau-regime neoclassical transport, sensitive to toroidal and helical magnetic-field harmonics and designated by the DD as the plateau factor of Stroth et al., equation (25); it characterizes magnetic geometry, not flattening of a density or temperature profile. Unit: 1. This token is not registered.

The named plateau factor belongs to magnetic-configuration and neoclassical transport characterization. The worklist inference that it is a profile-flattening measure is not adopted. Primary stellarator-confinement work discusses a geometrical factor for plateau flux, and the TJ-II study treats plateau factor as a neoclassical figure of merit. Neither is substituted for the precise normalization of the DD-cited Stroth equation, whose full text was not recovered. Keep r_eff/r_eff_LCFS = 2/3 as a stellarator effective-radius binding, not an assumed tokamak minor-radius coordinate.

Ruling requested: Consider the lexical physical base plateau_factor with its named neoclassical meaning, not a generic plateau qualifier. Recover and verify the cited equation before admission; keep the effective-radius convention in the binding.

  • plateau: A generic plateau qualifier does not define the named neoclassical magnetic-configuration measure.
  • peaking_factor: Describes profile shape, whereas this plateau factor describes magnetic configuration and neoclassical transport.

Measure and limits

The artifact gate checks seven unique token rows and every assigned worklist source path; exact leaf and parent documentation; membership of each reused token; strict parse/compose equality and recorded IR; the three proposed-token absences; all nine recorded refusals; distinct grid/line domains and their OR predicate; intact versus shattered pellet identity; current-source role; DD units; and binary operand locus scope. It uses four known-valid parser names and two known-present worklist paths before interpreting absence.

The gate runs against the main checkout parser at the recorded revision, using the repository’s shared environment. No grammar or test files are changed. No mutation control applies to this proposal-only node; the per-row parser probe is the declared check. The companion record contains proposed names, not a claim that source bindings have already been implemented in imas-codex.
